Microcontrollers - ESP32

What is a microcontroller?
A microcontroller is a small computer in an integrated circuit that can be programmed to perform specific functions.
In the real world, things like robot arms and devices related to the Internet of Things are connected to 
and operated by a microcontroller. (Insert pictures of robot arms and IoT device)

In this class, we will use an ESP32. (Insert picture of an ESP32)

Functionally the same as an Arduino, but ESP32 has superior onboard memory, which is what enables data to be 
written to SD cards smoothly with much lower risk of data loss.

This was an issue in the previous class, where every team used an Arduino Nano and everybody reported issues
with their SD card & reader.

ESP32 can be programmed with the Arduino IDE, so if you don't have that on your computer already you will want to
download that.

THIS IS IMPORTANT!

I said ESP32s are functionally the same as Arduino, however, you will need to install a driver in order for the
Arduino IDE to actually see that the ESP32 is connected to your computer for communication.
