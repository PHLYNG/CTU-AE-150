# Payload Structure

Rather than walkthrough a particular way of building a payload structure, this section will instead give the user ideas on different ways to approach the topic.

After conducting my own HAB flights and observing several others, generally speaking cubes of some sort are a reliable way to go. One could build a cube with foam core board and duct tape. Or one could use a premade styrofoam cube and cut it to serve specific needs.

3D printing can also add a new dimension to payload structures where one can design a mounting structure that serves a specific purpose, like holding a piece of perfboard in place. One can also design a 3D printed insert that slides into the center of a styrofoam cube.
